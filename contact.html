Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services

What is your delivery policy?

We offer free delivery on orders over 500 SAR within Riyadh. For other cities, delivery charges apply. Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days, while express delivery (1-2 days) is available for an additional fee.

Do you offer assembly services?

Yes! We provide professional assembly services for all our furniture. Assembly fees vary by product complexity and are calculated at checkout. Our trained technicians will assemble your furniture in your home.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for unused items in original packaging. Custom or personalized items cannot be returned. Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility unless the item is defective.

Do you offer warranties?

All our furniture comes with a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering manufacturing defects. Extended warranties are available for purchase. Warranty terms vary by product category.
