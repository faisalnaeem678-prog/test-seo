## Image Specifications

### Recommended Image Sizes:
- **Product Images**: 800x600 pixels (4:3 ratio)
- **Hero Images**: 1200x800 pixels (3:2 ratio)
- **Category Images**: 600x400 pixels (3:2 ratio)
- **Thumbnail Images**: 300x200 pixels (3:2 ratio)
- **Blog Images**: 800x500 pixels (16:10 ratio)

### Image Quality Requirements:
- **Resolution**: Minimum 72 DPI, recommended 150 DPI
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **File Size**: Under 500KB per image
- **Background**: White or transparent background preferred
- **Lighting**: Well-lit, professional photography
- **Angles**: Front view, side view, and detail shots

### Image Naming Convention:
- Use descriptive names with hyphens
- Example: `modern-sectional-sofa-3-seater.jpg`
- Include product category in filename
- Use lowercase letters only
- No spaces or special characters
